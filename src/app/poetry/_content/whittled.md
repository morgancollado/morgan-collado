Whittled 
Down to the bone 
The length of my stomach 
Measured in the 
Calories 
I can consume 
–
Sharper 
She said there’s 
Something different about me 
An angularity 
That wasn’t there before 
The pounds melted off 
And so did my tolerance 
For bullshit 
–
My face was 
So round 
Moon shaped and soft 
I look harder now 
Older 
The bags under my eyes 
Pronouncing 
I’ve been awake a long time 
–
Dad 
Would still be alive today 
If he took 
What I’m taking
Longer 
Dead at 51 
Than we were alive
Together 
–
I’ve always been fat 
A fact of life 
Accepted if 
Not loved 
Now I’m entering thin 
Society 
And I get to live? 
–
Tatiana de la Tiera 
Also died 
At 51
She was fat 
So was my dad 
And I get to live 
–
Fat people
Suffer
Overwhelming bullshit 
How much do we tolerate 
To survive
How much of that daily 
Struggle
Breaks down
Our bodies
Our minds 
Our spirits 
Is it the fat that kills us? 
Or the world 
–
I’m grateful 
I love my body 
I look hot 
And I no longer
have that Gnawing hunger 
Not satisfied 
Eating brought relief 
Fleeting 
Never enough 
–
My mind is quieter now
I don’t obsess about the same things 
When I eat 
I get full 
When someone hurts me
Clear eyed I see
What is not going to change 
When things happen 
I accept 
–
What will life 
Be like 
When I lose access
This shit is expensive
And I don’t want to stop
