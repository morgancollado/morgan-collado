I want to be 
The first thing you 
Think about
As the sun
Crests over the mountain 
A clarion call of gold and umber
It took 8 minutes for that photon 
To get to Earth 
An eternity 
When I’m not with you 
-
I want to be 
The last thing you 
Think about 
As the moon sets 
The night unfurled
The mountain swathed 
A blanket clinging shadows sticky 
The notes of your bass 
Guitar 
Winging into a starlit sky 
-
I want to love you 
The way you want to be loved 
Love the way you love me 
A coming home when I hear from you 
Heat of the desert expanding when I see you
Goosebumps when I remember your scent
On my lips 
Your name
On my mind
Your face reflected
In the twinkle of my eye 
Sparked every time I enter you 
-
I want to fill 
Your days with poems 
Set down the shape 
Of your soul in verse 
Immortal and inscrutable 
Each line an attempt 
To bottle lightning
Riding the storm 
Ass spilling before me
Make the narrow
Wide
Etching myself
To the very core of 
You
-
I want to be filled
By your song 
Caress me 
Strumming my chords
A melody only you can pluck 
A muse musing 
Upon Aphrodite 
On the half shell 
Your firmness rising
A river vast 
To drink from
Overwhelming
-
I want to dance
A two step perreo
Bump and grind
The beat connecting us
In anticipation
For something deeper
Feel hardness slide slick
Off
Ache of closeness craving more
-
I want to worship
Every facet
Grow your seed in me
Until tree bears fruit
Each morsel a lesson in how
To love you better 
Rooted
In how you need to be held
-
I want to know you
Feel the sandpaper rasp of age
On your palm
Look into wrinkled eyes
Like mine
And see the life loved
And lived
Shared between us
