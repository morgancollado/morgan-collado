He said my eyes looked like
Winter
Eyes so cold they scalded
Eyes that could hack away
Limbs
With FrostBite
Eyes that could create that
Tingly
Sleepy
Warm feelings
That sets in before HypoThermic death
A death, he said,
That I will happily submit too
For only in dying
Are we reborn
