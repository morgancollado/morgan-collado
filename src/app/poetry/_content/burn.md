I burn
Darkly.
Opalescent
Flames dancing
On the tips of my eyes
Shedding shadows and shade
Across fields
Of white grass
That feed no one
–
They buried my heat
In a forest
of Glass and Steel
Hoping that I would
Fail to remember
The blood that kept
My ancestors hot
–
But even a cactus
Blooms
In the desert
