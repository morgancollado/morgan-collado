I feel him here
Thump da dump thump da dump
In that space where my heart is
Filling up my chest cavity
With Inspiration
Desire
Passion
Love
Like a Furnace
It keeps me warm
When I’m so far away
I feel him here
In the beating of my heart
Thump da dump thump da dump
Ticking away the moments
Before I can see him again
