Rough edges
Grate across each
Other’s
Heart
Words fall
Like drops
Out of your mouth
Into sore wounds
I never knew what love looked like
But you showed me
The inside of your flesh
And I remembered
My
Self
Re-membered
Love is only ever
What we give
–
The sun lies
Across the mountain
Paining the sky crimson and gold
I turn to see
Your face outlined
In scrub bush and nettle
Your lips the color of
Twilight
Qué es el cariño
Entre dos cuerpos
Constantly atropellado by the world
When the only sure thing
Is death?
Querido
Keep your palm pressed
Against mine
And don’t be afraid
Porque estamos
Juntos
