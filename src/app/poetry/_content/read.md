Every time
Those double checks 
Go blue 
From grey
My heart skips

Half way between us

The only way I have to touch you

By longing 
The days measured 
In the seconds till I 
Can see you next 
Holding myself 
Just so
To not fall apart

Forcefully
And I think of you

Dancing with this ache
